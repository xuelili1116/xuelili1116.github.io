01
AI-enabled workflow · 0→1 prototype

From campaign brief to multilingual ad copy.

A lightweight internal prototype built with Claude Code transformed a repetitive writing task into a guided creative workflow, while keeping human review in the loop.

The opportunity

Campaign teams repeatedly adapted the same brief across languages and brand voices. I mapped the handoffs, identified the repeatable decisions, then prototyped a tool that turned one brief into structured copy options in three styles.

The goal was not to automate judgment. It was to remove the blank page, standardize inputs, and give marketers more time to review nuance and performance context.

03
Creative operations · Testing system

Building a faster way to find winning creative.

Instead of treating creative as a one-off request, I built a repeatable production and testing cadence that translated campaign signals into the next round of assets.

The system

I produced static assets and flyers in Canva on a weekly campaign rhythm, then expanded the workflow to support 30 to 40 new ad variants per day. Each batch isolated a small set of variables, so results could guide the next iteration.

The operating principle was simple: form a hypothesis, ship enough variation to learn, cut weak performers quickly, and scale what the data supported.
